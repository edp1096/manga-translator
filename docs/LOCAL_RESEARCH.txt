Repository documentation and local research

Keep docs/ for model provenance, conversion instructions, and concise validation
summaries suitable for source distribution. Put local screenshots, manga samples,
raw API results, logs, experimental binaries, backups and one-off investigation
scripts under research/. The root /research/ directory is excluded by .gitignore
and is not included in source release archives.

Historical detail corresponding to docs/<topic>/RESULTS.txt is available locally
under research/<topic>/. Relative artifact names in older summaries refer to that
local directory. Those artifacts are not required to build or run the application.
Maintained regression tests remain under tests/ and control/tests/; their generated
evidence defaults to research/ and can be regenerated. Model-provenance documents
and their conversion scripts remain in docs/model-provenance/.

2026-10-28 relocation moved files without deleting their contents. The local move
manifest and original hashes are in research/migration-2026-10-28.json. Active
investigation state (including the unfinished ch84 display work) is preserved in
research/<topic>/WORK_STATE.txt where present. Relocated investigation scripts
retain their directory depth and have project-local evidence paths updated.

Already ignored runtime/build/download folders data/, models/, runtime/, dist/
and archive/ retain their established locations and purposes.
