Outside dialogue display switch — 2026-10-28

User request: change tooltip versus white-backed translation immediately from
an extension panel toggle, without opening settings or retranslating.

Implemented:
- Panel checkbox switch (role=switch), labelled 말풍선 밖 대사·독백.
  Off=툴팁; On=흰 배경. Separate from existing balloon-white display selection.
- Persists outsideTextMode (default tooltip), synchronizes other open reader tabs,
  applies to all translated pages regardless of page selection, and is inherited
  by new translations. Base translation results are not rewritten by presentation.
- Eligible: tooltip-only regular text outside detected balloon geometry.
  Existing direct text stays direct. Detector-classified effects, explicit manual
  edits, empty/truncated OCR, oversized boxes and text with unsafe overlap remain
  unchanged. Unknown/malformed geometry is not treated as proof of an outside area.
- White card covers the original text rectangle with 4px padding; it does not
  reconstruct underlying art. Uses measured glyph bounds and smaller fonts to fit.
  Tall narrow boxes cap font size to reduce excessive single-character wrapping.
- Export and editor previews share the presentation. Applying an editor preview
  strips automatic styles, preserving manual overrides and base translation data.
- Changing modes while ZIP export is running is deferred until export ends;
  stale geometry responses cannot re-enable an option the user has turned off.
